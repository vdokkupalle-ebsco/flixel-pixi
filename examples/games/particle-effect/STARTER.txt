Campfire particle-effect starter
================================

These files are a complete Particle Editor export that can be loaded by a
Flixel-Pixi game:

- campfire-effect.json is the portable composed-effect document.
- flame.svg, ember.svg, and smoke.svg are the textures referenced by its
  emitter layers.

Preload the JSON as text and the three textures with FlxAssets, then pass the
parsed document to FlxParticleEffect.fromAssets. The playable example in this
directory shows the complete setup.
